🔒 Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Balearic Webcams (balearicwebcams, accessible at jamieinmallorca-afk.github.io/balearic-webcams) handles your information.

This website does not collect or store any personal data directly. We do not require registration or login. We do not run our own analytics.

This site links to or may embed content from third-party services. These services may set their own cookies and collect data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

Cookies

We store a single cookie preference value in your browser's local storage to remember your cookie consent choice. No personal data is stored in this value.

Third-party affiliate links (Booking.com, GetYourGuide, Rentalcars.com) will set their own tracking cookies when you click through to their sites. You can reject non-essential cookies using the banner at the bottom of this page.

Webcam images

Live webcam images displayed on this site are sourced from publicly available feeds operated by third parties (DGT, visiona.conectabalear.com, SOCIB). We do not store, record, or process these images.
